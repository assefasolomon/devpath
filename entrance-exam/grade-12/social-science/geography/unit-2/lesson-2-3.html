🛡️ Adaptation, Mitigation & CRGE

We cannot stop all the effects of climate change, but we can reduce the damage. This lesson covers adaptation (coping with the changes) and mitigation (reducing emissions), with a focus on Ethiopia's Climate Resilient Green Economy (CRGE) strategy.

📖 Reading: ~20 min
🎯 Quiz: 5 questions
🎯
Introduction
What you will learn

Responding to climate change involves two main strategies: Adaptation (adjusting to the actual or expected climate and its effects) and Mitigation (reducing greenhouse gas emissions or enhancing sinks). International agreements like the Paris Agreement and the Kyoto Protocol guide these global efforts. Ethiopia is doing its part through its CRGE strategy.

🌱
Adaptation & Mitigation Strategies
Section 2.5 & 2.6
📌 Adaptation vs. Mitigation

Adaptation means preparing for the effects (e.g., building flood defenses, developing drought-resistant crops). Mitigation means reducing the causes (e.g., using solar power, planting trees).

  • Adaptation Examples: Irrigation systems, early warning systems for floods, changing farming practices.
  • Mitigation Examples: Transitioning to renewable energy, improving energy efficiency, reforestation.
  • International Agreements: The Kyoto Protocol (1997) set binding emission targets for developed countries. The Paris Agreement (2015) aims to limit global warming to well below 2°C (ideally 1.5°C).
🇪🇹
Ethiopia's CRGE Strategy
Section 2.7
📌 Climate Resilient Green Economy (CRGE)

Ethiopia's strategy launched in 2011 to achieve middle-income status by 2025 while developing a green economy. It aims to keep greenhouse gas emissions low while growing the economy.

  • Four Pillars of CRGE:
    1. Improving crop and livestock production.
    2. Protecting and re-establishing forests.
    3. Expanding renewable energy generation.
    4. Adopting energy-efficient technologies in transport and industry.
  • Green Legacy Initiative: A massive national tree-planting campaign to combat deforestation and climate change.
  • Renewable Energy: Ethiopia relies heavily on hydropower (like the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam) and is exploring wind and geothermal energy.

Summary
Key takeaways from Lesson 2.3
What you should remember
  • Adaptation prepares us for climate effects, while mitigation reduces greenhouse gas emissions.
  • The Paris Agreement is a major global treaty aimed at limiting temperature rise.
  • Ethiopia's CRGE strategy aims to build a green economy and reduce emissions.
