📘 Unit 6 · MoE 2023 Curriculum · Pages 122–145

Poverty and Inequality

Poverty and inequality are interrelated concepts — and a daily reality for millions of people worldwide. This unit explores how poverty is defined and measured, the nature of inequality, global and regional patterns, the situation of women, Ethiopia's specific poverty picture, and the role of indigenous knowledge.
