Unit 8 · Post-1991 Ethiopia · MoE Textbook §8.3–8.4

🌍 Socio-Economic Issues & Peacekeeping

Since 1991, Ethiopia has faced significant socio-economic challenges, including poverty and ethnic tensions. However, it has also emerged as a major contributor to international peacekeeping missions across Africa.

📖 Reading: ~25 min
🎯 Quiz: 5 questions
🎯
Introduction
What you will learn

The post-1991 period has been a time of both progress and struggle for Ethiopia. The country has experienced rapid economic growth but remains one of the poorest in the world. It has also played a vital role in maintaining peace and security in Africa, contributing troops to several United Nations and African Union peacekeeping missions.

📈
Socio-Economic Issues
Section 8.3
  • Economic Growth: Ethiopia has seen rapid GDP growth, driven by agriculture, construction, and services. The government has invested heavily in infrastructure (roads, railways, dams).
  • Poverty: Despite growth, a large portion of the population still lives in poverty, especially in rural areas.
  • Ethnic Tensions: The federal system based on ethnicity has been both a strength and a source of tension, with periodic conflicts over land, resources, and political power.
  • Health and Education: The government has made progress in expanding access to education and healthcare, but quality and accessibility remain challenges.
  • Gender Equality: There have been efforts to promote women's rights and representation in government.
🕊️
Ethiopia's Peacekeeping Role
Section 8.4
📌 Ethiopia in Peacekeeping

Ethiopia is one of the largest troop-contributing countries to UN and AU peacekeeping missions. Ethiopian forces have served in conflict zones including Liberia, Rwanda, Sudan, and Somalia.

  • Liberia: Ethiopian troops served in the UN Mission in Liberia (UNMIL).
  • Rwanda: Ethiopia contributed troops to help stabilize the country after the 1994 genocide.
  • Sudan: Ethiopia has been involved in peacekeeping in Darfur and the disputed Abyei region.
  • Somalia: Ethiopian troops have been a key part of the AU Mission in Somalia (AMISOM), fighting against Al-Shabaab.
  • Reputation: Ethiopia's peacekeeping contributions have earned it international respect and strengthened its diplomatic standing.
