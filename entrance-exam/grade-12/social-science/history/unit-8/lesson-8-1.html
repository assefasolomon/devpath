🗳️ Transitional Government & the 1995 Constitution

The fall of the Derg in 1991 opened a new chapter in Ethiopian history. This lesson covers the establishment of the Transitional Government, the drafting of the 1995 Constitution, and the creation of Ethiopia's federal system.

📖 Reading: ~25 min
🎯 Quiz: 5 questions
🎯
Introduction
What you will learn

When the Derg regime collapsed in May 1991, the Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Democratic Front (EPRDF) took control. They established a Transitional Government and promised a new democratic order. In 1995, a new constitution was adopted that created a federal system based on ethnic lines, fundamentally restructuring the Ethiopian state.

🏛️
The Transitional Government
Section 8.1
  • EPRDF Takeover (1991): The EPRDF, led by Meles Zenawi, assumed power and established the Transitional Government of Ethiopia (TGE).
  • 1991 National Conference: A conference was held in Addis Ababa to establish a transitional charter and prepare for elections.
  • Charter: The transitional charter recognized the right of ethnic groups to self-determination and established a multiparty system.
  • Eritrea's Independence (1993): Eritrea gained independence from Ethiopia after a UN-supervised referendum. Ethiopia became a landlocked country.
📜
The 1995 Constitution
Section 8.2
📌 The 1995 Constitution

Adopted in August 1995, it established the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia (FDRE). It created a federal system with 9 regional states (based on ethnic lines) and 2 chartered cities (Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa).

  • Federalism: The 9 regional states were created based on ethnic and linguistic lines, each with its own regional government.
  • Self-Determination: The constitution granted ethnic groups the right to self-determination, including the right to secede (though this has never been exercised).
  • Human Rights: The constitution guarantees a wide range of human rights, including freedom of speech, religion, and assembly.
  • Parliamentary System: Ethiopia adopted a parliamentary system with a Prime Minister as head of government and a President as ceremonial head of state.

Summary
Key takeaways from Lesson 8.1
What you should remember

  • The Transitional Government prepared the country for a new constitution.
