✊ The 1974 Revolution & The Rise of the Derg

In 1974, a combination of famine, economic crisis, and mass protests brought down the 2,000-year-old Ethiopian monarchy. This lesson covers the revolution, the fall of Haile Selassie, and the violent rise of the military Derg regime.

📖 Reading: ~25 min
🎯 Quiz: 5 questions
🎯
Introduction
What you will learn

By early 1974, Emperor Haile Selassie's government was in crisis. The Wollo famine, soaring oil prices, and widespread corruption had destroyed the regime's legitimacy. A series of strikes and protests by students, teachers, and workers paralyzed the country. On September 12, 1974, a military committee known as the Derg (Amharic for "committee") deposed the emperor and took control, ending the Solomonic dynasty.

🔥
The 1974 Revolution
Downfall of the Emperor
  • Trigger: The 1972-73 Wollo famine and the government's cover-up sparked outrage.
  • Strikes and Protests: In February 1974, teachers, taxi drivers, and students went on strike. Riots broke out in Addis Ababa.
  • Mutiny: Army units began to mutiny over pay and poor conditions, further weakening the emperor's control.
  • The Fall: On September 12, 1974, the Derg deposed Haile Selassie, arrested him, and abolished the monarchy.
⚔️
The Rise of the Derg
Military Rule and Radical Change
📌 The Derg

A Marxist-Leninist military junta that ruled Ethiopia from 1974 to 1991. Led by Mengistu Haile Mariam, it imposed state control over the economy and violently suppressed all opposition.

  • Initial Reforms: The Derg imprisoned the nobility and initially gained public support by abolishing the monarchy and the feudal system.
  • Execution of Leaders: On November 23, 1974, the Derg executed 60 high-ranking officials of the old regime, including the former prime minister and generals.
  • Socialism: The Derg declared Ethiopia a socialist state, aligning itself with the Soviet Union and Cuba.

Summary
Key takeaways from Lesson 6.2
What you should remember
  • The 1974 revolution was triggered by famine, corruption, and mass protests.
  • The Derg (military committee) deposed Haile Selassie on September 12, 1974.
  • The Derg adopted Marxism-Leninism and aligned with the Soviet Union.
  • On November 23, 1974, the Derg executed 60 high-ranking officials to eliminate opposition.
  • The monarchy was abolished, ending centuries of imperial rule in Ethiopia.
