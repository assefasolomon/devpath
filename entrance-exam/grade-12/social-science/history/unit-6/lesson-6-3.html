🔥 Derg Reforms & The Decline of the Regime

After taking power, the Derg introduced radical socialist reforms, but their brutal tactics and failed economic policies led to civil war, famine, and ultimately the collapse of the regime in 1991.

📖 Reading: ~25 min
🎯 Quiz: 5 questions
🎯
Introduction
What you will learn

The Derg's rule (1974–1991) was one of the most violent periods in Ethiopian history. They introduced sweeping socialist reforms, including land nationalization, but their authoritarian rule sparked fierce resistance. The "Red Terror" eliminated thousands of young people, while the 1984 famine and a devastating civil war eventually brought the regime to its knees.

🚩
Derg Reforms
Section 6.5
  • Land Nationalization (1975): All rural land was nationalized and distributed to peasants, abolishing the feudal system.
  • Nationalization of Industry: Banks, factories, and foreign businesses were taken over by the state.
  • Peasant Associations: The Derg created peasant associations to govern rural areas, which eventually became tools of control.
  • Resettlement Programs: The regime forcibly relocated millions of people, often resulting in death and suffering.
  • The Red Terror (1977–1978): To silence opposition (especially from students and rival leftist groups), the Derg launched a brutal campaign of mass arrests, torture, and killings. Tens of thousands were killed.
📉
The Decline and Downfall
Section 6.6
  • Civil Wars: The Derg faced armed resistance from multiple groups, including the Eritrean People's Liberation Front (EPLF) and the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF).
  • The 1984 Famine: A massive famine killed over a million people. The government's mishandling of aid and the ongoing war worsened the crisis.
  • Economic Collapse: The socialist policies ruined the economy, leading to food shortages and hyperinflation.
  • The Fall (1991): In May 1991, the EPRDF (led by the TPLF) and other rebel forces advanced on Addis Ababa. Mengistu Haile Mariam fled the country, and the Derg regime collapsed.

Summary
Key takeaways from Lesson 6.3
What you should remember
  • The Derg introduced land nationalization in 1975, ending the feudal system.
  • The Red Terror (1977–1978) saw the brutal suppression of political opponents.
  • Armed resistance from the EPLF and TPLF weakened the regime.
  • The 1984 Famine killed over a million people, worsening the crisis.
  • In May 1991, the EPRDF took Addis Ababa, Mengistu fled, and the Derg collapsed.
