🌟 Appreciation of Nature

Before we can apply biology to improve human life, we must first learn to appreciate the natural world that gives us everything — food, air, water, and life itself. This lesson is about seeing nature with a biologist's eyes.

📖 Reading: ~15 min
🎯 Quiz: 5 questions
🎥 Videos: 4 resources
🎯
Introduction
What you will learn

Think about the last time you stopped to notice something beautiful in nature — a sunrise, a tree, a bird, or the sound of rain. Those moments are what this lesson is about: the appreciation of nature. But it's more than just enjoying beauty — it's understanding why nature matters, how biology helps us make sense of it, and what we can do to protect it.

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Define nature
  • Explain the relationship between nature and biology
  • Describe the role of biology in nature protection
  • Express and appreciate the beauty of nature
  • Recognize how nature supports human health and prosperity
📌
What is Nature?
MoE Textbook, p. 2
📌 Nature (MoE Textbook, p. 2)

Nature is the phenomena of the physical world including plants, animals, the landscape, and other features and products of the Earth.

📌 From the subatomic to the cosmic

Nature ranges from the subatomic (atoms, molecules) to the cosmic (stars, galaxies). It encompasses:

• Living things — plants, animals, microorganisms, humans
• Geological processes — mountains, rivers, volcanoes, soil
• Weather and climate — rain, sun, wind, seasons
• Physics — matter, energy, forces

Nature & Biology
The relationship
📌 How biology connects to nature (MoE Textbook, p. 2)

As a field of science, biology helps us understand the living world and how various species (including humans) function, evolve, and interact.

What Nature Provides Us

Nature provides us with the essentials of life. According to the MoE Textbook, nature gives us:

🍎
Food

The food we eat — from crops and livestock to fish and wild plants.

💨
Air

The air we breathe — purified by plants and ocean phytoplankton.

💧
Water

The water we drink and irrigate our crops with, cycled continuously.

🌳
Goods & Services

Timber, medicine, clothing, materials — countless products derived from nature.

💚
Health

Nature-based medicines and physical well-being from natural environments.

😊
Happiness

Nature reduces anger, fear, and stress, and increases pleasant feelings.

📝 The mental health benefit of nature

According to the MoE Textbook (p. 2): "We benefit from viewing the scenes of nature to reduce anger, fear and stress and increase pleasant feelings." — this is one of the most important, yet often overlooked, ways that nature supports us.

🎨
The Beauty of Nature
Appreciating it in everyday life

Appreciating nature isn't complicated — it's a habit. According to the MoE Textbook (p. 3), we learn and appreciate the beauty of nature through:

Way to Appreciate NatureWhy It Helps
🌱 Planting a treeConnects you to the future — trees grow for decades
🤗 Hugging a treeReduces stress and reconnects you with the natural world
🚶 Walking in natureImproves physical and mental health
🌺 Growing flowersBeautifies your surroundings and attracts pollinators
📸 Photographing natureTrains your eye to see details you'd otherwise miss
🎨 Drawing or painting natureDeepens your connection through careful observation
📝 Writing about natureReflects your thoughts and feelings about the natural world

Nature & Human Health — MoE Findings

  • Mental health: Viewing scenes of nature reduces anger, fear, and stress and increases pleasant feelings
  • Physical health: Spending time in nature is linked to lower blood pressure and improved immune function
  • Cognitive health: Nature exposure improves concentration and reduces mental fatigue
  • Social health: Natural spaces bring communities together for recreation and shared experience
