Key Concepts — Mechanics

  • Equations of motion apply only for uniform (constant) acceleration
  • Newton's three laws: (1) Inertia, (2) F = ma, (3) Action-reaction
  • Projectile motion: horizontal velocity is constant; vertical is accelerated by g
  • Range is maximum at 45° for flat ground launches
  • Momentum is conserved in all collisions (elastic and inelastic)
  • Energy is conserved; total KE + PE stays constant (no friction)
  • Elastic collision: KE and momentum conserved; inelastic: only momentum conserved
  • Centripetal force is directed toward the center — it's the net force

Key Concepts — Fluids

  • Pressure is scalar — acts equally in all directions at a point
  • Atmospheric pressure at sea level = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa
  • Buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid; determines floating or sinking
  • Floating object: density less than fluid; sinks if greater
  • Pascal's principle: pressure change transmits undiminished through a fluid (hydraulic press)
  • Bernoulli: faster flow = lower pressure (venturi effect)
  • Continuity: narrower pipe → faster flow (mass conservation)
  • Ideal fluid: incompressible, non-viscous, steady, irrotational flow

Key Concepts — Thermal Physics

  • Temperature = measure of average kinetic energy of particles
  • Heat = energy transferred due to temperature difference
  • Absolute zero = 0 K = −273.15 °C (no molecular motion)
  • Three modes of heat transfer: conduction, convection, radiation
  • Latent heat of fusion: solid ↔ liquid; vaporization: liquid ↔ gas
  • 1st law of thermodynamics: energy conservation (ΔU = Q − W)
  • 2nd law: entropy of an isolated system always increases; heat flows from hot to cold
  • Carnot efficiency: maximum possible efficiency of any heat engine
🌡️ Constant Reminders
  • All gas law calculations must use Kelvin, not Celsius
  • Water's specific heat c = 4186 J/(kg·K) — memorize this
  • Latent heat of fusion of water = 3.34 × 10⁵ J/kg; vaporization = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg
  • At STP: 1 mol gas = 22.4 L

Key Concepts — Waves & Optics

  • Transverse waves: oscillation ⟂ to propagation (light, water surface)
  • Longitudinal waves: oscillation ∥ to propagation (sound)
  • Sound travels faster in solids > liquids > gases; cannot travel in vacuum
  • Light is electromagnetic; travels at c in vacuum
  • Reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection
  • Refraction: light bends when entering a different medium
  • Total internal reflection occurs when angle > critical angle (fiber optics)
  • Convex lens converges; concave lens diverges
  • Real image is inverted, formed on opposite side; virtual image is upright
🔍 Sign Conventions (Mirrors & Lenses)
  • f > 0 for concave mirrors & convex lenses
  • f < 0 for convex mirrors & concave lenses
  • u > 0 for real objects (almost always)
  • v > 0 = real image (same side as object for mirrors, opposite for lenses)
  • v < 0 = virtual image

Key Concepts — Electricity & Magnetism

  • Like charges repel, unlike attract — Coulomb's law follows inverse-square
  • Electric field lines point from + to −; denser where field is stronger
  • Current is flow of charge: conventional current flows + → − (opposite of electron flow)
  • Resistance depends on material (ρ), length (L), and area (A): R = ρL/A
  • Kirchhoff's laws: (1) Current into junction = current out; (2) Sum of voltages around a loop = 0
  • Right-hand rule for magnetic field around a current-carrying wire
  • Fleming's left-hand rule for the force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
  • Faraday: changing magnetic field induces an EMF (basis of generators, transformers)
  • Lenz's law: induced current opposes the change that produced it
