⚠️ Safety and High Pressure

High pressure systems power our daily lives — LPG cylinders, pressure cookers, industrial washers, and compressors. But they also carry serious risks. This lesson covers the physics, applications, and safety measures for high pressure.

📖 Reading: ~18 min
✏️ Examples: 5 worked

Introduction
What you will learn

High pressure is any pressure far greater than 1 atmosphere (usually > 50 atm). It has many applications — from cooking food faster to cleaning large surfaces with jets. But mismanaged high pressure can cause catastrophic accidents. Understanding physics keeps us safe.

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • List applications of high pressure
  • Identify components of high-pressure equipment
  • Recognize the hazards of high-pressure systems
  • Apply safety measures for LPG cylinders and pressure washers
  • Understand Pascalization for food preservation

5.1 Applications of High Pressure

📌 Common Applications

• Pressure cookers — cook food faster at higher temperatures
• LPG gas cylinders — store fuel at high pressure
• Car and bicycle tires — inflated to 30–40 psi
• Pressure washers — clean hard surfaces efficiently
• Compressors — increase gas pressure for tools, sprayers
• Physical science research — study material properties at extreme pressures
• Pascalization — food preservation by high-pressure processing

5.2 Components of High-Pressure Equipment

📌 Key Components

• Compressors / pumps — increase gas or fluid pressure by reducing volume
• High-pressure piping — tubes, valves, fittings designed for high stress
• High-pressure vessels — storage tanks, chambers built to contain pressurized fluids
• Safety accessories — safety valves, bursting discs, pressure switches
• High-pressure instrumentation — pressure, temperature, flow and level sensors

5.3 Hazards of High-Pressure Systems

⚠️ Main Hazards

• Blast injuries — from explosion of pressure vessel
• Flying debris — parts of failed equipment
• Contact injuries — with hot steam or released liquids
• Fire — from escaping flammable gases
• Chemical exposure — if pressurized contents are toxic

⚠️ Common Causes of Failure

• Damaged equipment or faulty design
• Poor or no maintenance
• Unsafe work practices
• Operator error (lack of training)
• Incorrect installation
• Inadequate repairs or unauthorized modifications

5.4 Safety Measures — LPG Gas Cylinders

📌 Home LPG Safety

✓ Keep the cylinder upright on a flat surface
✓ Store in a well-ventilated area
✓ Protect from physical impact or damage
✓ Keep flammable materials away (kerosene, gasoline)
✓ Always turn off the knob after use
✓ Keep the knob out of reach of children
✓ Educate people around you on safety
✓ Don't use for long hours while cooking

5.5 Safety Measures — Pressure Washers

👁️
Eye Protection
  • Wear safety glasses or goggles
  • Prevent flying projectiles entering your eye
🥾
Foot & Hand
  • Use work boots
  • Wear proper safety gloves
  • Most common injury: hands
🔊
Hearing Protection
  • Wear ear protection for gas-powered washers
  • Especially for extended use
🚫
Never Aim At
  • Pets or people
  • Could cut the skin
  • Even from a distance
🧍
Proper Stance
  • Stand properly when using
  • Turn off machine & water before removing hoses
🔧
Equipment Check
  • Inspect before each use
  • Check for leaks or damage
  • Never bypass safety valves

5.6 Pascalization — Food Preservation

📌 High-Pressure Food Processing

Pascalization (also called high-pressure processing, HPP) uses very high pressure to
• Diminish or cancel microorganism activity
• Extend shelf life of perishable foods
• Preserve flavors and nutrients better than heat treatment

Applied to: juices, fish, meat, dairy products.

Worked Examples
5 exam-style problems solved step by step
Example 1Pressure Cooker Physics
Problem: Why does food cook faster in a pressure cooker?
Physics
Higher pressure inside the sealed cooker raises the boiling point of water.
Effect
Water boils at ~120°C instead of 100°C — cooking food faster.
✅ Pressure cooker: higher pressure → higher boiling point → faster cooking.
Example 2LPG Cylinder Safety
Problem: Why must LPG cylinders be kept upright and in a well-ventilated area?
Upright
LPG inside is a mix of liquid and gas. If tilted, liquid (not gas) can escape through the valve — dangerous.
Ventilation
If LPG leaks, it accumulates in enclosed spaces. Ventilation prevents buildup that could explode.
✅ Upright keeps liquid inside; ventilation prevents dangerous accumulation.
Example 3Why Pressure Washers Cut Skin
Problem: A pressure washer directs water through a small nozzle. Explain why it can cut skin.
Continuity
Water forced through a small nozzle speeds up dramatically.
Pressure
The high-speed jet carries high kinetic energy concentrated on a small area.
Result
The kinetic energy is transferred on impact — enough to cut tissue.
✅ High speed × small area = high impact pressure → can cut skin.
Example 4Car Tire Pressure
Problem: A car tire reads 32 psi where atmospheric pressure is 14.3 psi. What is the absolute pressure?
Formula
P_abs = P_gauge + P_atm
Substitute
P_abs = 32.0 + 14.3 = 46.3 psi
✅ Absolute pressure = 46.3 psi
Example 5Pascalization for Juice
Problem: Why is Pascalization preferred over heat treatment for fruit juices?
Heat treatment
Kills microbes but also destroys vitamins and flavors.
Pascalization
High pressure inactivates microbes without heat.
Result
Preserves fresh flavor and nutrients better than thermal processing.
✅ Pascalization keeps juice fresh-tasting while still extending shelf life.
