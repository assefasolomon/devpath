Question 1
The 5th term of an arithmetic sequence is 17 and the 9th term is 33. What is the common difference?
  • A 3
  • B 4
  • C 5
  • D 8
Answer: B

Using the arithmetic sequence formula: aₙ = a₁ + (n−1)d
a₅ = a₁ + 4d = 17
a₉ = a₁ + 8d = 33

Subtract: (a₁ + 8d) − (a₁ + 4d) = 33 − 17
4d = 16
d = 4
Question 2
Evaluate: lim(x→2) (x² − 4) / (x − 2)
  • A 0
  • B 2
  • C 4
  • D Does not exist
Answer: C

Direct substitution gives 0/0 (indeterminate).

Factor the numerator: x² − 4 = (x − 2)(x + 2)
Cancel: (x − 2)(x + 2) / (x − 2) = x + 2
Substitute x = 2: 2 + 2 = 4
Question 3
Find the derivative of f(x) = 3x⁴ − 2x² + 5x − 7.
  • A 12x³ − 2x + 5
  • B 12x³ − 4x + 5
  • C 12x³ − 4x
  • D 3x³ − 4x + 5
Answer: B

Apply the power rule to each term:
d/dx(3x⁴) = 12x³
d/dx(−2x²) = −4x
d/dx(5x) = 5
d/dx(−7) = 0

Combine: f'(x) = 12x³ − 4x + 5
Question 4
Evaluate: ∫₁³ (2x + 1) dx
  • A 8
  • B 9
  • C 10
  • D 12
Answer: C

Find the antiderivative: F(x) = x² + x
Evaluate at upper limit: F(3) = 9 + 3 = 12
Evaluate at lower limit: F(1) = 1 + 1 = 2
Subtract: 12 − 2 = 10
Question 5
A fair die is rolled once. What is the probability of getting a number greater than 4?
  • A 1/6
  • B 1/3
  • C 1/2
  • D 2/3
Answer: B

Sample space: S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}, n(S) = 6
Event: numbers greater than 4 = {5, 6}, n(A) = 2

P(A) = n(A) / n(S) = 2/6 = 1/3
